ABOUT US

We're reimagining
how buildings think.

Chiara Core was founded on a simple belief: buildings should be intelligent, sustainable, and effortless to manage — for everyone from the CEO to the facilities manager.

OUR MISSION

Making buildings
work smarter
for everyone

We started Chiara Core because we saw an industry still relying on disconnected, legacy systems that made building management harder than it needed to be. Our mission is to unify building operations into one intelligent platform — reducing costs, carbon, and complexity for every building type, everywhere.

Today, Chiara OS is trusted by property owners, facility managers, and sustainability teams across commercial, retail, corporate, education, and healthcare estates.
